# Posting cheat sheet (GTA VI fan page)

## Weekly rhythm (launch week: 1 post/day + 1 Reel/day)
| Day | Format | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Reel (checklist / countdown) | reach (new people) |
| Tue | Carousel (save-worthy guide) | saves + shares |
| Wed | Poll poster (A or B) | comments |
| Thu | Fact poster ("confirmed vs unknown") | trust + shares |
| Fri | Reel | reach |
| Sat | Meme-style quote poster | tags / shares |
| Sun | Carousel recap + CTA | follows |
Post 4-5 times a week minimum. Consistency beats volume. Best time: test 12-2pm and 8-10pm IST for 2 weeks, keep what wins (Insights > Audience > Most active times).

## Formats
- **Poster**: 1080x1350 (4:5). One idea, <=6 words headline. Goal: share/comment.
- **Carousel**: 1080x1350, cover hook + 4 slides + CTA. Goal: SAVE. Swipe-through is the strongest signal.
- **Reel**: 1080x1920 (9:16), 15-30 s. First 2 seconds = hook. Voice-over + captions on screen (people watch muted). Goal: reach.

## How to post (mobile)
1. Send the files to your phone (Drive/WhatsApp to self). 2. Instagram > + > Reel (or Post). 3. Select file(s) (carousel: select multiple in order). 4. Paste caption from captions_and_hashtags.txt. 5. Add cover frame (Reels: Edit cover). 6. Turn on "Share to Facebook" if you want cross-reach. 7. Post, then reply to every comment in the first 30 minutes.
Add original/trending audio only from Instagram's own library (do not upload game music or trailer audio).

## Engagement rules
- Ask ONE clear question per post (A or B, "tag your co-op partner").
- Reply to comments within 30-60 min; ask a follow-up question in the reply.
- Hashtags: 5-8 relevant (#GTA6 #GTAVI #GamingIndia ...). No #fyp/#viral spam.
- Put a "Save this" and "Send to your squad" CTA on every carousel/Reel.
- Watch Insights weekly: keep formats with highest saves + shares per reach; drop the rest.
- Collab/Stories: reshare comments, use poll/quiz stickers daily.

## Safe-content rules (avoid takedowns/strikes)
- Do NOT use trailer clips, screenshots, logos, character art, or game music. Only original graphics like this kit.
- Do NOT repost the ~30 big gaming channels' videos. Add your own value: checklists, polls, explainers, original edits.
- Facts only from facts.json (Rockstar newswire). Label rumours clearly as unconfirmed or skip them.
- Keep the "Fan page, not affiliated" line. Don't name the page with Rockstar/GTA official-sounding words ("Official").

## Growth ideas that fit a graphics-only page
- Weekly "Confirmed vs Rumour" carousel. - Squad polls. - "Things to do before launch" checklists. - Memes in the same HUD style (new quote posters). - Series with a number ("Day 3 of launch week") so people follow for the next one.
- Collaborate: comment usefully on creator posts, DM smaller creators for shout-for-shout.

## Money (honest)
No income is guaranteed. Typical route: grow followers/engagement for months, then brand deals (gaming gear, accessories), affiliate links (controllers, headsets), or Instagram's bonus programs where available. Check Meta's current eligibility rules before planning around any payout.

## 30-day growth plan (no guarantees, this is what usually works)
- Days 1-3: set up profile (clear niche in bio: "GTA VI updates, polls & checklists | India"), 9 posts ready before you start.
- Daily: 1 Reel + 1 poster/carousel (use the 09:00 IST pack). Alternate Reel hooks: "Before you play GTA 6, do this", "3 things nobody told you".
- Daily 20 min: comment genuinely on 10 posts from gaming pages/creators in your niche; reply to all your comments; 3 Stories (poll sticker).
- Launch week 19-26 Nov is the biggest search/interest spike: post every day, use Stories countdown, post fresh facts only from Rockstar newswire.
- Week 2+: repeat what got the most saves/shares; make a series ("Day X"); try 1 collab/shoutout swap per week.
- Never buy followers or use follow-unfollow bots: they kill reach.
